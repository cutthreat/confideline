## Reconstructed Operating Regulations

### Reactivation / Lift

1. Lift is mainly for previous buyers or strong leads.
2. If a chat is exhausted today, stop and return tomorrow.
3. The system should support a next-day lift reminder.
4. Do not keep pushing a client forever in the same day.
5. Rotate angles if the same client returns repeatedly.

### AI And Translation

1. AI may be used for drafting interpretations or idea generation.
2. AI output must be adapted by the expert.
3. Translators can be used for unclear client messages.
4. If one translator gives strange output, verify with another.
5. Translation confidence should affect whether the expert asks a clarifying question.

### Paid Conversion

1. Do not fully answer in free chat.
2. Use a hook that can be expanded in paid session.
3. `Book Now` should follow a clear reason and intrigue.
4. "No money" is an objection, not an automatic end.
5. Some clients need several hours or until the next day before they buy.

### Client Psychology

1. Many clients buy emotional support, hope, and attention.
2. Repeated same-topic clients can be regular revenue.
3. The expert should not shame repetition.
4. Positive framing and belief-building are core parts of the service.
5. Training includes psychology, empathy, and studying successful phrasing.

### Safety

1. Self-harm-like messages must trigger a safety path.
2. Supportive language alone is not enough for a product workflow.
3. The system should require escalation/support resources and mark the chat as high risk.

### End Of Shift

1. Finish active high-value chats.
2. Decide which chats stay for tomorrow.
3. Do not overwork clearly unsuitable chats.
4. Send dashboards/performance screenshots to mentor.
5. Count sessions, revenue, hours, and quality notes.
