## Executive Summary

Day 7 Part 1 has two layers:

1. **Graduation / onboarding operations** after the training week: self-work hours, questionnaire, identity verification, schedule, salary, bonus calls, manager communication, and first independent shifts.
2. **Continued live cabinet supervision**: reactivation, previous buyers, favorites, comments in client cards, cross-expert purchases, duplicate/new chats, Book Now handling, and the rule that an expert must finish chats they take into work.

The most important new product signal is that the competitor platform has a real operational lifecycle around a trainee becoming a working expert. This includes:
- required accumulated hours;
- a blocking questionnaire attached to the account;
- identity/location verification;
- scheduled HR/payroll/bonus calls;
- self-managed shift availability;
- mentor review of chats before final acceptance;
- dashboard/hour counting as acceptance evidence.

## Reconstructed Operating Regulations

### Training Completion

1. Trainee must complete required work hours, around 36 hours for the training stage.
2. Add schedule buffer when closing final hours.
3. Final questionnaire appears inside the platform and blocks chat visibility until complete.
4. Questionnaire collects identity, location, social links, language skills, photo, and document screenshot.
5. If the questionnaire does not close, check for future booked sessions and cancel them if needed.
6. After training, trainee has HR/payroll/schedule call and a bonus-system call.

### Work Schedule And Pay

1. Days off are flexible; weekends are not mandatory off-days.
2. Full-month target is around 160-180 hours.
3. Full rate mentioned is around $14/hour.
4. If hours are not completed, pay is reduced proportionally.
5. Power outages and emergencies are acceptable only with communication and reasonable mitigation.
6. Manager communication is mandatory; disappearing without explanation is not acceptable.

### Client Continuity

1. Previous buyers should be added to Favorites.
2. Comments/notes in client cards should be read before working the client.
3. A negative note from another expert is not absolute truth.
4. Purchase history is a strong signal even if the client used multiple experts.
5. Duplicate/new chats by the same buyer are normal and should not be treated emotionally.

### Chat Ownership

1. If an expert takes or is assigned a chat, they must work it.
2. Accepted/assigned chats are not optional.
3. Active sessions and active chats should be considered before breaks.
4. Mentor review checks whether trainees actually finish what they took.

### Conversion

1. Returning clients should often go into lift/reactivation, not a full new free reading.
2. If the client knows how to book, send the button.
3. If the client asks another question, empathize and move to a paid session.
4. Coupon availability depends on client status; old users may not get new-user discounts.
5. Sensitive topics should not be answered directly in free chat.

## Key Product Takeaway

Day 7 Part 1 expands the system from chat conversion into workforce operations. The competitor workflow connects expert onboarding, identity verification, training hours, schedule, pay, bonus education, manager communication, and live chat ownership into one operating model.

For Confideline, the admin workspace should not treat expert chat work as isolated messages. It should track:
- expert onboarding state;
- required/completed hours;
- questionnaire completion;
- schedule exceptions;
- previous-buyer retention memory;
- cross-expert client history;
- assignment responsibility;
- sensitive-topic safeguards.
